How Merra Network Keys Work

1
Generate your key

Your organization's network key is a secure identifier that authorizes other Merra tenants to send you transactions.

2
Share with partner organizations

Provide your key to your customers or vendors so they can link your company to their contacts book.

3
Direct cross-organization transactions

When they create an invoice for your contact, the Merra Network validates your active subscription key and dispatches transactions directly.

4
Complete control & revocation

As long as you keep your key active, subscriptions remain valid. If you revoke a key, incoming transactions using it are blocked immediately.

Give this key an optional label (e.g. "Primary Network Key" or "Vendor Portal Key") to easily identify it later.
